The Fuxan Os Ventos International Chess Open 2026 will be held at the prestigious Círculo das Artes de Lugo, located at Praza Maior, 23 (27001 Lugo).

You can reach by Google Maps → Location

The Círculo das Artes is one of the most traditional cultural and social institutions in the city of Lugo. Its building, located in the heart of Praza Maior, is an emblematic venue in the historic centre and offers an elegant and representative setting for the tournament.

The playing hall will provide a calm, bright and comfortable atmosphere for players and arbiters, allowing all rounds of the international championship to run smoothly.

Thanks to its privileged location in the centre of Lugo, participants will be just a few minutes’ walk from a wide range of hotels, restaurants, cafés, shops and some of the city’s main tourist attractions, such as the Roman Wall, a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

The organisation has chosen this venue in order to offer a high-quality playing experience, combining excellent facilities with one of the most representative locations in Galicia.

We look forward to welcoming players and accompanying visitors to a unique venue that will help make the Fuxan Os Ventos International Open 2026 an unforgettable experience.
